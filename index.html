A Poem To You


I still find pieces of you in my room that I walk through,
In the memories I can't leave behind no matter what I do.
The late-night conversations and the dreams we would share,
And the quiet moments on the phone when I feel you there.

We could sit for hours just looking in each other's eyes,
Watching every spark and every light we couldn't disguise.
The silence now feels heavier because I know the truth and I see,
That my own choices built the distance standing now between you and me.

If I could undo the moments that broke your trust apart,
I'd gather every shattered piece and mend your precious heart.
Not because I'm afraid of losing you or being left alone,
But because you were my safest place, the closest friend I've known.

No one has ever known my soul the way that you do,
No one saw the broken parts of me and loved me through.
I remember your smile, our inside jokes, the comfort of your hand,
The way being with you felt like home in a world I couldn't understand.

I've met so many people since then, but none could ever compare,
Because what made you beautiful was the love you chose to share.
I know apologies can't erase the tears I've caused to fall,
They can't undo the lonely nights or heal the hurt of all.

But there's one thing I hope you always carry as you go,
It's that our love and friendship were real, more than you'll ever know.
And if this is truly goodbye, then with gratitude I'll start,
Carrying the years, the laughter, and your memory in my heart.

Wherever life may take us and whatever paths we choose,
I will always love you, and a piece of you will forever be apart of me and vice versa.

Thank you for being my person, my confidant, and my heart ❤️🔐
